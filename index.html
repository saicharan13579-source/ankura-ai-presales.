The 2-minute summary

  1. Speed decides who wins the buyer. Contacting a lead within an hour makes it nearly 7× as likely to qualify (Harvard Business Review). Most companies are far slower.
  2. Indian rules allow this, with care. Promotional calls are limited to 9 AM–9 PM IST. A callback the buyer asks for isn’t unsolicited, and inbound calls can be answered any time.
  3. Others already sell this. EnableCalls (Hyderabad) offers AI calling agents in Telugu, Hindi and English at ₹3.50–6.80 per answered minute.
  4. Building our own costs less. About ₹2.7 a minute at published rates, so roughly ₹1.2 lakh a month for 6 full-time AI agents, and Ankura owns the data.
  5. The upside is large because our tickets are large. Our villas list at about ₹2–5.5 Cr, so one extra sale can cover years of running cost.
  6. Calls come from our own number, shown as “Ankura Homes”. India’s caller-name display (CNAP, from 1 Nov 2026) and Truecaller verified badges mean buyers see who is calling. The app refuses any other caller ID.
  7. It’s already built and tested. The working software handles calls, 4 languages, NRI time zones, retries, a dashboard and Google Sheets.

Study 1: Harvard Business Review, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads” (March 2011). Researchers audited 2,241 US companies by sending test web leads. Only 37% responded within an hour, 16% took 1–24 hours, 24% took more than 24 hours, and 23% never responded.

Study 2: MIT / InsideSales lead-response study (2007). This was a separate, earlier study, often mixed up with the HBR one. It found the odds of reaching a lead were about 100× higher when called within 5 minutes rather than 30, and the odds of qualifying it were about 21× higher.

What it means for Ankura: a buyer on a portal enquires with several developers at once, often in the evening. A callback the next working day starts the conversation after competitors have already called.

6What we built and tested
The working software behind Priyaread more ↓
  • Phone: works with Plivo, Twilio or Exotel; inbound and outbound calls; live transfer to a sales manager
  • Voice: Sarvam AI speech for Telugu, Hindi, English and Marathi, switching mid-call; option to use a cloned voice
  • Lead sources: website form, Meta lead ads, Instagram DMs, 99acres / MagicBricks / Housing.com via webhook, old-lead CSV campaigns

  • Sales hand-off: when a returning buyer shows more interest, connects them live to that project’s sales person (on duty, right language, same person as before) after a WhatsApp brief
  • Rules built in: never claims to be human (honest if asked), consent, buyer-local calling hours, “call me now” night callbacks, do-not-call list, duplicate check, 3 retries
  • NRI buyers: time zone from phone number, callbacks at their chosen local time, video walkthrough booking
  • Behaviour: patient presales persona, gentle check-ins on silence, AI “tone check” after every call
  • Tracking: live dashboard, full transcripts, AI summary and hot/warm/cold score, Google Sheets
  • Tested: 34 automated tests pass; a simulated call runs end to end. Real phone calls need our accounts and keys, which is the pilot’s first step.
